## 💡 What AROS Does

* Detects stalled deals and pipeline risks automatically
* Prioritizes opportunities based on impact and urgency
* Recommends next-best actions using AI
* Executes workflows across systems (Slack, CRM, outreach)
* Tracks outcomes and revenue impact in real time

---

## 🧠 Core Capabilities

* Executive Command Center (real-time pipeline visibility)
* AI Decision Engine (actionable recommendations)
* Funnel Monitor (risk scoring across deals)
* Action Layer (automated execution)
* Activity Feed (continuous tracking of outcomes)
